5. CONCLUSION
A new controller for resolved acceleration control of
robotic manipulators was proposed for the trajectory
tracking of the serial robotic manipulator using screw theory and concepts from spatial dynamics. Representation
of the motion variables with dual quaternions allowed
coupled treatment of translation and orientation components of trajectory tracking error. A comparison with a
decoupled controller reveals better orientation tracking
while achieving comparable performance for the translation components. However, a more thorough analysis is
